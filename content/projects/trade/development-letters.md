# 客户开发信模板（6 封）

> 原则：短、具体、只问一个问题。第一封不要写超过 120 个英文单词。
> 每封都替换 [ ] 里的内容，**不要**原样群发——外贸客户对模板极其敏感。

---

## 第 1 封 · 首次触达

**Subject:** [Product name] supplier for [Buyer Company] — quick question

Dear [Mr. Weber],

I noticed [Buyer Company] distributes [product category] in [Germany].
We are a manufacturer of [your product] based in [City], China, and supply several
importers in [Europe / North America].

Could you tell me which specification matters most to you — [option A] or [option B]?
I can send our best price for that version right away.

Best regards,
[Your Name] | [Company] | [WhatsApp / Tel]

---

## 第 2 封 · 价值主张（第 3 天）

**Subject:** Re: [Product name] — 3 details buyers usually ask about

Dear [Mr. Weber],

Following up with three things our European clients ask about most:

1. MOQ: [100 pcs], mixed models allowed
2. Lead time: [25-30] days; our record this year is [21] days
3. Certificates: [CE / RoHS / REACH]

Which one would you like the details on?

Best regards,
[Your Name]

---

## 第 3 封 · 案例分享（第 7 天）

**Subject:** How [similar company in same country] cut their cost by [12%]

Dear [Mr. Weber],

Short case: [Similar Buyer] had a problem with [late delivery / inconsistent quality].
We solved it by [specific action], and their unit cost dropped about [12%] in six months.

Happy to share the one-page summary if it is useful.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

---

## 第 4 封 · 报价引导（第 14 天）

**Subject:** A price for [Product name] — just need one number

Dear [Mr. Weber],

I would like to send a firm quotation, but I need only one number from you:
your expected annual volume.

With that I can tell you exactly what we can do on price and payment terms.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

---

## 第 5 封 · 样品邀约（第 30 天）

**Subject:** Sample of [Product name] — we cover the courier

Dear [Mr. Weber],

If it helps you evaluate, we can send a free sample and cover the courier cost.
You only need to confirm the address.

Would that work for you?

Best regards,
[Your Name]

---

## 第 6 封 · 长期跟进（第 60 天，此后每季度一次）

**Subject:** [Product name] update — new [model / certificate] available

Dear [Mr. Weber],

Quick update from our side: we released [new model] and obtained [new certificate].
Attached is the one-page spec.

If your sourcing plan changes this year, I would be glad to quote.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

## 常见错误清单

- [ ] 标题里出现 "Best price"、"Dear Sir or Madam"、"Cooperation" —— 直接被判为垃圾邮件
- [ ] 第一封就塞 8 个附件
- [ ] 正文超过 150 词
- [ ] 结尾问了 5 个问题
- [ ] 没有署名、公司、联系方式
- [ ] 用图片代替文字（容易被判垃圾邮件，且手机端看不清）
